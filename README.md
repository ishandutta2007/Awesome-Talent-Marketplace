# Awesome-Talent-Marketplace

## Top Talent Marketplace Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Internal Talent Mobility, Skills-Based Matching, Opportunity Marketplaces, Career Pathing & Workforce Agility*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Talent Marketplaces**. These systems match employees to internal roles, projects, gigs, mentorships, and learning opportunities using skills inference, AI matching, and career-pathing logic — unlocking internal mobility and reducing external hiring dependency.

**Examples** include Gloat, Fuel50, Eightfold AI, Phenom, Workday Talent Marketplace, SAP SuccessFactors Opportunity Marketplace, 50skills, Neobrain, 365Talents, and TechWolf (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Fully featured enterprise-grade open-source talent marketplaces are rare. This section is expanded with the strongest available open-source skill-matching platforms, mentorship marketplaces, HR matching tools, and related projects that organizations can adapt for internal mobility use cases.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Gloat](https://gloat.com/)**  
  Leading internal talent marketplace platform focused on skills-based matching of employees to roles, projects, gigs, and career opportunities at enterprise scale.

- **[Fuel50](https://fuel50.com/)**  
  Career pathing and talent marketplace platform emphasizing skills ontologies, employee-driven mobility, and retention-focused career experiences.

- **[Eightfold AI](https://eightfold.ai/)**  
  AI-native talent intelligence platform covering external hiring and internal mobility with deep skills inference and career trajectory modeling.

- **[Phenom](https://www.phenom.com/)**  
  Talent experience platform that unifies recruiting and internal mobility, matching employees and candidates using skills and experience data.

- **[Workday Talent Marketplace](https://www.workday.com/)**  
  Native opportunity marketplace within Workday HCM that surfaces internal roles, projects, and gigs to employees based on skills and preferences.

- **[SAP SuccessFactors Opportunity Marketplace](https://www.sap.com/products/hcm/successfactors.html)**  
  Opportunity and talent marketplace capabilities inside SAP SuccessFactors for internal mobility, projects, and career development.

- **[50skills](https://50skills.com/)**  
  Skills-based talent and workforce platform supporting matching, mobility, and skills visibility.

- **[Neobrain](https://www.neobrain.io/)**  
  AI-driven talent and skills platform offering marketplace, workforce planning, and skills ontology capabilities.

- **[365Talents](https://www.365talents.com/)**  
  Skills-centric talent marketplace and career development platform with strong European presence and multilingual support.

- **[TechWolf](https://techwolf.ai/)**  
  Skills intelligence platform that powers talent marketplaces and workforce decisions with real-time skills data and inference.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Self-hosted job boards and internal opportunity portals built on open CMS or low-code platforms.
- Graph databases modeling people–skills–roles relationships for advanced matching.
- Open learning platforms (Moodle, etc.) integrated with skills tagging for learning-to-opportunity pathways.
- Employee profile and resume parsing open libraries.
- Simple recommendation engines using collaborative or content-based filtering for internal opportunities.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with an open HR or profile system, add a skills taxonomy (open or custom), implement semantic matching with vector embeddings, and surface opportunities via an internal portal or chatbot. Use open workflow tools for applications and approvals, and feed results into analytics dashboards. This approach works well for mid-sized organizations or as a complement to existing HCM systems, though it lacks the deep enterprise skills inference, bias testing, and scale of commercial talent marketplaces.

## Disclaimer
- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Talent marketplace systems process sensitive employee data and influence career opportunities. Open-source solutions provide transparency and ownership but require careful attention to data privacy, bias mitigation, access controls, and integration with formal HR processes.
- Always ensure compliance with employment law, data protection regulations, and internal equity policies when deploying any matching system.
